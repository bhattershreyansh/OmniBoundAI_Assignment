For years, I thought that cable and cellular companies had the least customer friendly pricing policies. Then Hubspot (and other enterprise SAAS) came along and said "hold my beer."

I've had several experiences that have reinforced this and this recent one is no exception.

Here's the recent story:
I get hired to help a company clean up their marketing. I look at their hubspot billing and they are paying double their normal contracted rate because of additional Marketing Contact tiers. Most of these contacts are from an old CSV someone at the company uploaded to keep in the CRM and the client has no understanding they are being charged for contacts they aren't actively marketing to. I work with the client to reduce the number of contacts by about 30,000. After our next billing cycle, I notice their billing rate hasn't changed. I research and contact support to find out that Hubspot will happily increase your bill based on MC volume automatically, but they will not decrease your bill unless you specifically request it. No notifications or hints that you could be saving a little money each month. So I reach out through support to inquire about the process. I get an answer a couple days later with a new "quote" and the sales rep tells me we must sign it that same day because our billing cycle starts tomorrow. With less than a 24 hour turnaround, I am unable to get the client to sign the new contract (which also requires the client to input a payment option even though they are on an autobilll - this is a big part of the reason the contract could not be signed with such a quick turnaround because the accounting team was not available). I explain the situation and request a small grace period for the client to sign the next day. The sales rep tells me that is impossible, and instead offers a "great" deal to save when we move to an annual plan. The "savings" offered is off of the original price before the reduction which is less than the current bill, but 40% higher than what the reduce bill should be. This last part could be an honest mistake, but the collective billing policy and ethos at hubspot is just so bad - almost predatory.

I'll use this as a cautionary tale to future clients debating a Hubspot subscription. Hubspot is a solid CRM, but in a world with so much SAAS parity, a company that cares a little more about their customer experience than their bottom line will win my recommendation.
