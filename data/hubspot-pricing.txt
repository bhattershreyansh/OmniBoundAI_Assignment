HubSpot Sales Hub Pricing & Packaging (2026)

Free Plan:
- Price: $0/month (Free for up to 2 users, no credit card required)
- Includes: Free marketing, sales, service, content, data, and commerce tools.
- Key features: Contact management, deal pipelines, quotes, meeting scheduling, email tracking, live chat, reporting dashboard.

Starter Plan:
- Price: Starts at $15 - $20/month per seat (billed annually) or $20/month per seat (monthly)
- Target: Essential marketing, sales, service, content, and data management software for small teams.
- Key features: Removes HubSpot branding, includes simple automation, email templates, repetitive task queues, basic reporting, call recording, and 500 HubSpot Credits.

Professional Plan:
- Price: Starts at $90 - $100/month per seat (billed annually, 5 seat minimum = $450 - $500/month) plus a mandatory one-time onboarding fee (~$1,500).
- Target: Comprehensive sales software to automate and scale sales processes.
- Key features: Advanced sales automation (sequences, workflows), deal progression, custom reporting, forecasting, quoting with e-signatures, coaching playlists, lead scoring, and 3,000 HubSpot Credits.

Enterprise Plan:
- Price: Starts at $150/month per seat (billed annually, 10 seat minimum = $1,500/month) plus mandatory onboarding fee (~$3,500).
- Target: Advanced control and customization for scaling organizations.
- Key features: Custom objects, hierarchical teams, advanced permissions, predictive lead scoring, recurring revenue tracking, conversation intelligence, and single sign-on (SSO).

Key Pricing Considerations & Upsells:
- Marketing Contacts: If bundling with Marketing Hub, pricing scales significantly based on contact count tiers.
- Seat Model: HubSpot distinguishes between View-Only users and Core/Sales Seats with edit and workflow permissions.
