Pipedrive CRM Pricing & Plans (2026)

Lite Plan:
- Price: $14/seat/month (billed annually, $168/seat/year) or $24/seat/month (billed monthly)
- Target: Small teams and solo reps getting organized.
- Key features: Lead, calendar, and pipeline management; simple contact activity tracking; 500+ app integrations; AI-powered basic report creation; meeting intelligence preview.

Growth Plan:
- Price: $24 - $39/seat/month (billed annually) or $44/seat/month (billed monthly)
- Target: Teams that want to automate email communication and follow-ups.
- Key features: Customizable email sync with 2-way tracking; automated workflows (up to 30 active automations); meeting scheduler; recurring revenue tracking; basic sales forecasting.

Premium Plan:
- Price: $59/seat/month (billed annually) or $79/seat/month (billed monthly)
- Target: Teams requiring deeper AI insights, revenue forecasting, and data enrichment.
- Key features: AI sales assistant; smart data enrichment; multiple dashboards and advanced revenue forecasting; formula fields; document and contract signing e-signatures.

Enterprise Plan:
- Price: $99/seat/month (billed annually) or $129/seat/month (billed monthly)
- Target: Larger sales organizations needing custom permissions and unlimited capacity.
- Key features: Unlimited custom fields and reports; advanced user permissions and visibility groups; dedicated account manager; phone support; complete security center with audit logging.

Key Add-ons:
- LeadBooster: $32.50/company/month (Chatbot, Live Chat, Web Forms, Prospector tool).
- Campaigns (Email Marketing): Tiered pricing based on subscriber volume.
